HOW IT WORKS

From Search to Service in Simple Steps

Our platform digitizes hiring, verification and booking so households and helpers have a clear process.

01. Register

Create a household account or join as a helper.

02. Search

Browse verified helpers and filter by service, experience and availability.

03. Choose a Plan

Select hourly, monthly or yearly service pricing.

04. Request Booking

Send your requirements and preferred start date.

05. Accept

The helper accepts or rejects the service request.

06. Review

Track service history and leave feedback after completion.
